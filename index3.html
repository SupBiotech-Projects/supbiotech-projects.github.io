About SBPJ

The SupBiotech Projects Journal provides a formal space for publishing high-quality project work, scientific syntheses, experimental reports, and perspectives from students, researchers, teachers, and collaborators connected to SupBiotech.

Scientific training

Helping students learn how to structure, write, review, and communicate scientific work.

Project visibility

Giving biotechnology projects a durable and citable public presence.

Community journal

Connecting students, alumni, faculty, researchers, and industrial partners.

Aims and scope

SBPJ welcomes contributions related to biotechnology in its broadest sense, including experimental, computational, industrial, environmental, health-related, and societal dimensions.

Research articles

Original student or team projects with clear methods, results, and interpretation.

Reviews

Structured syntheses on current topics in biotechnology and life sciences.

Perspectives

Short articles on innovation, ethics, entrepreneurship, policy, or emerging technologies.

Submit to SBPJ

Authors are invited to submit manuscripts prepared in a clear scientific format. Submissions may include project reports, review articles, methodological notes, data-driven analyses, or perspectives.

1. Prepare

Write your manuscript with title, abstract, introduction, methods, results, discussion, and references.

2. Review

Submissions are reviewed for clarity, scientific structure, originality, and educational value.

3. Publish

Accepted articles are published online as part of an SBPJ issue.
